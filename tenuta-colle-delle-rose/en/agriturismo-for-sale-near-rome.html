Good to know

The stable and barn roofs are asbestos-cement and must be removed by a licensed contractor before those buildings are converted. As with any Italian farmland sale, neighbouring farmers may have pre-emption rights and must be notified before completion. We work with English-speaking notaries and advisers and can guide you through each step.

Frequently asked questions

Can a foreigner run an agriturismo in Italy?

Yes. An agriturismo must be run by an agricultural business, and here that business is included in the sale. Many foreign owners run it through the company with a local manager or partner.

How does buying property in Italy work?

You sign a purchase proposal, then a preliminary contract (compromesso) with a deposit, and finally the deed (rogito) before a notary. You need an Italian tax code (codice fiscale) and usually an Italian bank account.

How far is it from Rome airport?

About 50 km from Rome Fiumicino airport and 25 km from central Rome.
