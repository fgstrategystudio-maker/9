# Surface brief: app Dieta (Diario, Progressi, Corpo, Obiettivi)

Mode: Operate. Audience: one person (IT, Android/Chrome, PWA) tracking a weight-loss diet daily, several times a day, often one-handed at the table, light and dark.
Job: log food fast (text, voice, photo, barcode), see what's left today, check month progress and deficit, adjust goals.
Constraints: vanilla JS PWA, no build; data only in localStorage; palettes salvia/oceano/artico/oliva/grafite × light/dark stay selectable (user decision).

## Direction contract
THESIS: the category standard played straight (user chose "Stile attuale, riordinato"); the screen is a working tool of the Yazio/Lifesum class. Refuses the endless single-column feed: every tab answers one question, deeper material sits one tap away.
OWN-WORLD: incumbent world kept: tinted neutral grounds, white/near-black cards, one accent per palette, macro colour roles (prot/carb/fat), Manrope, drawn stroke icons for controls, food emoji as content.
STORY: open → see kcal left and what each meal contributed → add or fix a meal in one tap → check month and deficit in Progressi without scrolling past unrelated charts.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Diario: greeting, ring + macro bars, quick add bar; meals below each with a single + and a footer of Modifica/Salva. Progressi: sticky segmented sub-navigation (Panoramica · Alimentazione · Peso e bilancio) under the title.
FORM: canon (standing exit), seed: none (pinned) "Stile attuale, riordinato".
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
