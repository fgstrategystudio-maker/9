Business process consulting

Business process optimization: less friction, more margin.

As a company grows, its processes stop holding up. I put them back in order starting from how work really gets done, with concrete actions on sales, operations and data.

Request an audit → See the results
The service

Simpler processes, faster decisions.

The signs are almost always the same: long response times, recurring errors, information scattered across emails, spreadsheets and management software, overloaded key people. Each problem looks small on its own; together they cost margin and clients.

Business process consulting starts with a process audit: I map how work really gets done, measure times and handoffs, and identify the bottlenecks. Then we choose the actions with the best ratio between impact and effort.

Typical actions are concrete: simplifying the sales process and the handoff between marketing and sales, setting up a CRM the team actually uses, clarifying roles and responsibilities, automating repetitive steps, building a dashboard with a few indicators. Only then do we decide whether new tools are needed.

FAQ

Answers to the most common questions.

Where do we start?

With a process audit: team interviews, data analysis and mapping of the real workflows. It is the quickest way to understand where to act.

Do we need to change software?

Not right away. First you fix the process, then you choose the tool. The tools already in place are often enough, if used well.

How long does it take to see improvements?

The first simple actions can deliver results within the first few weeks; deeper changes take a few months, with indicators to verify them.

Does the team need to be involved?

Yes, always. The people doing the work know where time is lost, and a new process only works if people understand it and adopt it.

Does it work for service companies too?

Yes. Service, consulting, hospitality and e-commerce companies have different processes, but the same problems with handoffs, timing and data.
