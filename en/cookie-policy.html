Cookie Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This page sets out which cookies francescogizzi.com uses, what they are for and how to change your mind at any time. For the handling of personal data in general, see the Privacy policy.

1. Your choices

On your first visit a banner appears with three options: Accept, Decline and Customise, where you can switch each category on or off. Until you choose, no analytics or advertising cookie is installed, and the advertising pixel’s script is not even downloaded.

Your choice is stored in your browser. You can change it whenever you like:

If the button does nothing, cookies are blocked by your browser: in that case nothing is stored and there is nothing to manage.

Declining does not limit your use of the site in any way.

2. Necessary cookies

They make the site work and remember your choice. They need no consent and cannot be switched off. They are kept in your browser’s local storage (localStorage), are never sent to any server, and stay until you clear the site’s data.

3. Analytics cookies

Active only with your consent. They show which pages are actually read, where visitors come from and where they stop. The data is aggregated and is not used to identify you.

Provider: Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited, with processing also by Google LLC in the United States). Consent Mode v2 is in place: until you consent, Google receives no browsing data.

4. Marketing cookies

Active only with your consent. The ChatGPT Ads pixel (OpenAI) shows which visits come from adverts and which turn into an enquiry.

Until you consent the script is not downloaded: no data leaves your browser. After consent the code is loaded from bzrcdn.openai.com and events are sent to bzr.openai.com. The identifiers the pixel sets are managed by OpenAI and stored in your browser; if you withdraw consent the script stops being loaded and stops sending events.

5. Who receives the data

  • Google (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC, USA), usage statistics. Google’s privacy notice.
  • OpenAI (USA), measurement of advertising campaigns.
  • Vercel Inc. (USA), hosting: records access data (IP address, user agent) in technical logs, for security and reliability. These do not depend on cookie consent.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Transfers take place under the safeguards required by the GDPR (Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent arrangements).

6. Acting from your browser

Besides the banner, you can delete or block cookies in your browser settings: Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge. Block everything, though, and some features of other sites may stop working.

For statistics alone there is also Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on.

7. Changes

This page is updated when the tools the site uses change, or the rules that govern them do. The date of the last change is always at the top.
